Privacy Policy for Merchant

Effective date: October 28, 2026

This policy explains how the Merchant business management app ("Merchant", "we", "us") handles information when organizations and their authorized users use the app.

Information handled by the app

Depending on how an organization uses Merchant, information may include:

  • Account details such as a user's name, email address, authentication identifiers, organization membership, and role or permissions.
  • Business contact details entered for farmers, vendors, customers, and employees, such as names, phone numbers, and addresses.
  • Business records such as purchases, sales, invoices, inventory, commissions, expenses, payments, payroll, advances, balances, notes, and payment references.
  • Audit information associated with business activity, such as the user and time associated with a recorded change.

How information is used

Information is used to provide Merchant's business-management features, authenticate users, apply organization roles and permissions, display balances and reports, keep records synchronized, and help maintain and secure the service.

Cloud services and sharing

Merchant uses Supabase for authentication and cloud database services. Information entered into the app is sent to that service to provide the app. Supabase processes information under its own terms and privacy practices.

Merchant can create reports and documents for export or sharing. When a user chooses to export or share a document, its contents may be sent to the app or service the user selects. Merchant does not itself execute bank transfers or card payments; it records payment information entered by the organization, such as amount, payment method, and reference.

We do not sell personal information. Information may be disclosed when needed to provide or secure the service, comply with law, respond to valid legal requests, or when an organization or user directs a share or export.

Organization-managed information

Organizations control the business records entered by their users and manage access through user roles and permissions. If you are an employee, farmer, vendor, customer, or other person whose information appears in an organization's records, contact that organization to request access to or correction or deletion of those records.

Retention and your choices

Business records are retained to support the organization's use of Merchant. An organization administrator may manage users and records within the app. For account, privacy, or deletion requests that you cannot resolve with your organization administrator, contact us at yesuvadian@gmail.com. We will review requests and respond as required by applicable law. Copies retained in backups or required for security, dispute resolution, or legal compliance may not be removed immediately.

Security

We use technical and organizational measures intended to protect information. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. Users should protect their sign-in credentials and organizations should grant access only to authorized users.

Children

Merchant is a business-management app and is not directed to children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app or its practices change. The effective date above indicates when this version was published.

Contact

For privacy or data questions, contact yesuvadian@gmail.com.
